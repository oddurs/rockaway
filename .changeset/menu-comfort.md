Give menus comfortable sections (0317). `Menu`'s `comfort` puts half a row of air after each rule and section title when comfortable, or a row when spacious, beside the rule and never in it. A rule that would land on a half-row takes the half-row before it as well, so every rule stays on a whole row where the frame draws it, and the menu closes to whole rows. Menus stay compact by default, a terminal's menu. The rhythm table gains `group`, the air after a rule, and `menuLayout` lays a menu's rows out in half-rows.
