Add four surface levels, `bg.surface-sunken`, `bg.surface-base`, `bg.surface-raised` and `bg.surface-overlay` (`--rk-bg-surface-*`), a small step apart in every theme so panels read as layers. Sunset's are blues around its Atlantic ground at dusk and sands at golden hour. The contrast gate holds every text role on every level, and when text falls short on one it is the layer that gives up a step, so no existing colour moves. Forced colours collapse all four to Canvas.
