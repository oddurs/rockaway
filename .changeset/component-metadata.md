Publish every component's metadata as data: `@rockaway/react/metadata` (typed), `@rockaway/react/meta.json` and its JSON Schema, `@rockaway/react/meta.schema.json`. Each component gives its purpose, when to use it and when not to, its anatomy, its variants (read from its variant helper), its states (rows of the state vocabulary), its keyboard map, the props read from its source, the tokens read from its stylesheets, and its text snapshots. Adds `buttonBuffer`, Button as text.
