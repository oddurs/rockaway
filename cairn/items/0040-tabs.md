## Purpose

Switch between views that share one place: `files`, `log`, `diff`. Not for
navigation between pages (links), and not for steps in a sequence.

## Anatomy

`<Tabs>`, `<TabList>`, `<Tab>`, `<TabPanel>` on React Aria. The tab list is
drawn into the top edge of the panel's frame, so tabs and panel are one box:
`┌ files ─ log ─ diff ─────┐`, with the selected tab reverse video and bold.
Tabs that do not fit scroll by whole tabs, with `‹` `›` marks at the ends.

## States

`data-selected`, `data-focused`, `data-focus-visible`, `data-hovered`, `data-disabled`, `data-orientation`.

## Tokens consumed

`border.default`, `fg.default`, `fg.muted` (unselected tabs), `bg.inverse`, `fg.on-inverse`.

## Accessibility

`tablist`, `tab` and `tabpanel` through React Aria: arrows move between tabs,
Home and End jump, activation automatic by default and manual by prop. The
frame and the overflow marks are `aria-hidden`.
