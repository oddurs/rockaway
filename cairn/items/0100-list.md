## Purpose

The selection primitive a TUI leans on: a viewport exactly `rows` cells tall
that scrolls, and snaps, in whole rows; a cursor and a selection drawn as two
signals; type-ahead; and a scrollbar drawn by the engine in a one-cell column.
Behaviour is React Aria's `ListBox`. Polished against the contract in 0133.

## Anatomy

- `List`: the viewport (`role="listbox"`) and the scrollbar beside it. The
  scrollbar's thumb is sized from the collection's row count, which rows report
  from inside React Aria's collection; `total` overrides it for a list that does
  not hold every row.
- `ListItem`: a row (`role="option"`). The row has one reserved mark cell for
  the cursor and, under `selectionMode="multiple"`, a second one for the check.
  Then comes the label, clipped at the viewport's edge. The marks are
  `aria-hidden` and come from the theme (`useGlyphs()`).
- Empty state: through `renderEmptyState`, the `empty` text (default "Nothing
  here.") in `fg.muted`, after the reserved cells.
- `listBuffer` draws all of it as cells, and is the text snapshot.
  `ListItem` draws its marks with `listMarks`, and the scrollbar is
  `scrollbarBuffer`, the same functions `listBuffer` calls. Stories check that
  `screenshot()` of the rendered list equals `listBuffer`.

## States

Every state comes from the 0118 vocabulary, and none changes a row's size:

- hover (`data-hovered`): the label is underlined
- cursor (`data-focused`): the cursor mark in the first reserved cell, and
  nothing else
- selected (`data-selected`): reverse video, made by swapping the list's own
  `fg.default` and `bg.surface` so that forced colors keeps it. Under
  multi-select it also gets the check mark in the second reserved cell.
- disabled (`data-disabled`): dim (`fg.disabled`). A disabled row that is also
  selected is reversed in `fg.disabled`.

## Tokens

`--rk-fg-default`, `--rk-bg-surface`, `--rk-fg-muted`, `--rk-fg-disabled`,
`--rk-attribute-underline-offset`, `--rk-cell-width`, `--rk-cell-height`, as
extracted into the metadata from `list.css` and the painter.

## Accessibility

- **Name:** `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` on `List`. A row's name is its
  label; the marks are `aria-hidden`.
- **Keys:** up and down, Home and End, Page Up and Page Down, Space and Enter
  to select (toggle under multi-select), Shift with an arrow to extend, Mod+A
  for every row, Escape to clear, and type-ahead. A plain-text label is the
  type-ahead text without a `textValue`.
- **Announced:** selection comes from `aria-selected`. The scrollbar is
  `aria-hidden`; the rows carry the position.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Cursor glyph plus reverse video, so selection survives forced colors and colour blindness
- [ ] Virtualised by row, and the scrollbar is drawn in cells
- [x] Keyboard: arrows, home and end, page keys, type-ahead

## TUI criteria (added by the pivot, cairn 0076)

- [x] Sized in cells, and drawn by the frame engine: no box characters written by hand
- [x] Frame glyphs are `aria-hidden`; the accessible name never contains one
- [x] Ships a text snapshot, which is its documentation as much as its test
- [x] Operable by keyboard alone, and usable with a finger at touch density
- [x] State reads without colour: an attribute or a mark carries it too
- [x] Conforms at `strict`, or declares its exception with a reason

## 2026-09-23

Selection is two signals, never one: a cursor glyph in its own cell and reverse video. Either alone is a single point of failure — the glyph is lost to a reader who cannot see it, the reverse is lost to a forced-colors mode that reverses everything. The cursor is aria-hidden, because '▸ src/index.ts' is not the name of anything.

## 2026-09-23

The scrollbar is a one-cell column drawn by the engine, so it snapshots as text and its whole behaviour fits in five lines: thumb at the top, thumb walking down, thumb at the bottom, full thumb when there is nothing to scroll, and one cell minimum however long the list. It takes a row count rather than the DOM, which is what will let 0115 virtualise underneath it without touching it.

## 2026-09-23

Virtualisation is NOT done, and its criterion is left unticked rather than reworded: it moved to 0115 with everything learned. React Aria's Virtualizer does virtualise, but a keyboard jump to an unrendered row (End, or type-ahead across a long list) leaves focus nowhere. Correct keyboard behaviour is the point of a TUI list, so the Virtualizer came out and the rows all render for now. Ruled out along the way: scroll-snap, an onScroll prop shadowing the virtualiser's own handler, and static versus dynamic collections.

## 2026-09-23

A grid item that scrolls needs grid-template-rows: minmax(0, 1fr) and min-block-size: 0. Without them the row takes its height from a thousand list rows, the box never scrolls, and — while the Virtualizer was still in — it saw every row as visible. Worth remembering for any component with a viewport.
